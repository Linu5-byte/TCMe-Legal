# TCMe Community Guidelines

Effective date: [Insert Date]

Contact: [tcme.support@gmail.com]

TCMe is a close-friends app. These guidelines help keep the app safe, respectful, and useful.

## Be Respectful

Do not harass, threaten, bully, shame, or target other users. Do not encourage others to do so.

## No Hate or Abuse

Do not post or send hateful, demeaning, or abusive content based on protected characteristics such as race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, disability, gender identity, sexual orientation, age, or similar attributes.

## No Inappropriate or Exploitative Content

Do not share sexually exploitative content, non-consensual intimate content, graphic violence, or content that is illegal or unsafe.

## No Spam or Manipulation

Do not spam posts, comments, messages, friend requests, reports, or group chats. Do not use TCMe to scam, impersonate, deceive, or manipulate other users.

## Respect Privacy

Do not share another person’s private information without permission. Do not repost private messages, photos, or personal details in a way that harms or exposes another person.

## Use Reports Responsibly

You can report users, posts, and comments. Reports are reviewed manually. Use reporting when something violates these guidelines or makes the app unsafe. Do not abuse the reporting system.

## Close-Friends Expectations

TCMe is designed for people who have mutually connected. Even within a friend network, users should communicate respectfully and avoid sharing content that others would not reasonably expect.

## Consequences

If content or behavior violates these guidelines, TCMe may take action. Actions may include reviewing reports, removing content, limiting features, removing friendships, or disabling accounts.

## Account Deletion and Safety Records

Users may delete their accounts in the app. Account deletion removes account-related data as described in the Privacy Policy. Reports may be retained in anonymized or limited form for safety and moderation purposes.

Contact support at [tcme.support@gmail.com].
