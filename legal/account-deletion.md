# TCMe Account Deletion Information

Effective date: June 4, 2026

[Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md) | [Terms of Service](terms-of-service.md) | [Account Deletion](account-deletion.md)

This page explains how account deletion works in TCMe.

Contact: tcme.support@gmail.com

## How to Delete Your Account

You can delete your account from inside the TCMe app.

To delete your account:

1. Open TCMe.
2. Go to your Profile screen.
3. Open the profile options menu.
4. Choose Delete Account.
5. Confirm the action and enter your password when asked.

Account deletion is intended to be permanent. After deletion, you may not be able to recover your account, profile, posts, messages, photos, or other account data.

## Data Deleted During Account Deletion

When account deletion is successful, TCMe deletes or removes account-related data, including:

- Profile information
- Profile photo
- Posts
- Photo posts
- Comments by you
- Comments on your posts
- Likes by you
- Likes on your posts
- Friend requests involving you
- Friendships involving you
- Direct messages involving you
- Group messages sent by you
- Group memberships involving you
- Uploaded profile images
- Uploaded post images
- In-app notifications involving you
- Related account data and local app cache

For group chats, TCMe removes you from group memberships and deletes messages you sent. TCMe does not automatically delete an entire group for all members just because you created it.

Reports may be kept in limited or anonymized form for safety, moderation, legal, or abuse-prevention purposes.

## Uploaded Images

TCMe deletes uploaded image files that belong to the deleted account where possible, including profile images and post images stored for that user.

TCMe does not intentionally delete shared default placeholder images or files that do not belong to the deleting user.

## Backups and Temporary Copies

Some deleted information may remain in backups, logs, or temporary technical copies for a limited time before automatic removal. These copies are not intended to remain available in the active TCMe app.

## After Deletion

After successful account deletion, TCMe signs you out and clears local app data where possible.

If you want to use TCMe again later, you may need to create a new account.
